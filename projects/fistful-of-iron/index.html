02 / Modern Western / College short film

Fistful of Iron

Joseph Garcia refuses to sell the family farm he inherited. Ramiro Gomez has other plans for the land—and the buried gold beneath it.

Role
Writer and cast (Ketterman)
Timeline
In development

Context

Set around Palmer, Texas, Fistful of Iron follows Joseph, his brother-in-law Ketterman, and a fight over family land that leads to a ten-pace duel on Sutton Road.

ScreenwritingPre-productionCinematographyEditing

What it's about

A family farm, buried gold, and a deal Joseph won't take.

Joseph Garcia inherits his grandfather's farm, along with a ledger and a Colt Walker. Somewhere on the land is buried gold.

Ramiro Gomez arrives with an astonishing offer to buy the property. Joseph refuses, and the fight over the farm pulls Joseph, Ketterman, and Gomez toward a ten-pace duel on Sutton Road.

Current status

The film is still being written and developed. The farm, the buried gold, Gomez's offer, and the Sutton Road duel are established parts of the story.
