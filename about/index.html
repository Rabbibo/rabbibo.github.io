RL

This is a studio, a workshop, a server room, and an archive—depending on what the day asks for.

Rab's Labs is where I keep the things I make and the systems I run: photography, short films, car projects, servers, and whatever I decide to try next.

The recovered site documents personal work and systems maintained for friends. It does not present unverified client history, testimonials, metrics, or awards.

Working principles

  1. 01

    Make the complex legible.

    Systems and stories both benefit from structure that people can follow.

  2. 02

    Keep the texture.

    Polish should clarify the work without sanding away its personality.

  3. 03

    Claim only what is true.

    Missing facts stay visibly missing until the source exists.
